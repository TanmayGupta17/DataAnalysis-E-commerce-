# Dataset Selection Document

## Business Domain: E-Commerce

### Why E-Commerce?
E-commerce is a rapidly growing sector with rich transactional data that enables 
analysis of customer behavior, operational efficiency, and marketplace dynamics. 
Understanding these patterns helps optimize revenue, customer satisfaction, and 
seller performance.

## Data Source
**Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset**
- **Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce
- **Provider:** Olist (Brazilian marketplace platform)
- **License:** Creative Commons (publicly available)
- **Time Period:** September 2016 - September 2018
- **Size:** 9 interconnected CSV files, 100K+ orders

## Raw Data Description

The dataset contains 9 tables:
1. **Orders:** Order status, timestamps, delivery dates
2. **Customers:** Customer location (state, city, zip code)
3. **Order Items:** Products per order, price, freight cost
4. **Products:** Product categories, dimensions, weight
5. **Reviews:** Customer ratings (1-5 stars), review text
6. **Sellers:** Seller location information
7. **Payments:** Payment type, installments, values
8. **Geolocation:** Latitude/longitude coordinates
9. **Category Translator:** Portuguese to English translations

## Business Questions to Answer

1. **Which product categories drive highest customer satisfaction?**
2. **How does delivery performance impact customer reviews?**
3. **Which geographic regions show strongest sales and satisfaction?**
4. **What seasonal trends exist in order volume and revenue?**
5. **What distinguishes top-performing sellers from others?**

These insights will help inform:
- Product assortment decisions
- Logistics optimization
- Regional expansion strategy
- Marketing campaign timing
- Seller performance management
